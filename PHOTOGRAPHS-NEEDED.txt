WHEN YOU ARE READY:
  1. Make a folder called  images  next to index.html

  3. Tell me, and I will repoint the whole site at that folder in one go

Export them around 2000px on the long edge and about 250 KB each. The current
ones average 643 KB, which is heavier than a wedding site needs.
