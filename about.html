How We Work

We believe in the power of a personal connection. We spend time getting to know your narrative, your tastes, the wedding's customs and style, and your family long before the big day arrives.

The only way we can truly and meaningfully capture moments is by getting to know you first. By the time the wedding comes around we are not strangers with cameras — we already know who is likely to cry, and where to stand when they do.

The Process

From First Hello To Final Album

  1. 01

    We Meet

    A call or a coffee. We hear your story, your dates, your families, and what you want to remember most. No pressure, no sales pitch.

  2. 02

    We Plan

    A simple written plan — coverage, team size, timings, deliverables and cost — so there are no surprises on either side. A 30% advance reserves your date.

  3. 03

    We Photograph

    We work close and unobtrusively across every function, following the day as it actually unfolds rather than a shot list.

  4. 04

    We Deliver

    Your photographs and films within 21 to 60 days of your last event, depending on your package — on time, or 5% off. Then an album designed to be handed down rather than filed away.
