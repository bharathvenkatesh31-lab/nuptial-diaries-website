How this works

Drag any photograph to move it. The first one is where the album opens. Hover one to make it the cover, or to use it as one of the two small pictures under the cover on the Photography page.

To move several at once: click them to tick them — shift-click to tick a whole run — then drag any ticked photograph, and they all travel together.
